Game data saved on your device

Reality Debt does not require an account. The game saves your best score, run counts, ad timing, and sound preference on your device so they carry over between sessions. These records are stored locally and are not sent to the developer. They remain on your device until you delete the app or otherwise clear its data.

Advertising

Reality Debt uses Google AdMob to display ads and requests non-personalized ads. When the app requests or displays ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as your IP address, device or app identifiers, interactions with ads, and diagnostic or performance data. Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, detect fraud, and improve its services. Google handles and retains this information under its own policies.
